Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Snackbyte AI ("we", "our", "us") operates an automated content-publishing tool that posts our own short-form videos to social media platforms including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. This policy explains what data we handle.

1. Who this applies to

Our tool publishes content only to social media accounts that we own and control. We do not offer accounts, logins, or services to third-party end users, and we do not collect personal data from members of the public.

2. Data we access

To publish to our own accounts, our tool uses access tokens issued to us by each platform (for example, TikTok, Meta, and Google). These tokens are stored securely as encrypted repository secrets and are used solely to upload and publish our own videos and their captions. We do not read, collect, or store other users' personal information, messages, or contact lists.

3. How we use it

Access tokens are used only to authenticate with each platform's publishing API and post our videos. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

4. Data retention

We retain only the credentials required to keep publishing. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's developer or account settings, which immediately ends our access.

5. Third-party platforms

Content we publish becomes subject to the privacy policy and terms of the platform it is posted to (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok). Please refer to those platforms' policies for how they handle published content.
